2. Clinical hub
Encounters is the single hub. Everything clinical — visits, orders, results, billing — hangs off one encounter, not off patients and providers separately.

⚠️ HIPAA PHI summary:
This model contains Protected Health Information (PHI) as defined by HIPAA. Sensitive data includes: names, geographic sub-state info (addresses, rooms), dates (DOB, admission, discharge, etc.), contact info (email, phone), medical record numbers, medical conditions/allergies, financial data (charges, claims), and license numbers. Ensure appropriate access controls, encryption, and audit logging are in place.
