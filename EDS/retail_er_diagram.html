⚠️ PII / PCI summary:
This model contains Personally Identifiable Information (PII) and payment-related data. Sensitive data includes: names, addresses, contact info (email, phone, IP address), dates of birth, browsing behavior, order and payment amounts, and payment references. Ensure appropriate access controls, encryption, and audit logging are in place — especially around the payments and shipments datasets.
