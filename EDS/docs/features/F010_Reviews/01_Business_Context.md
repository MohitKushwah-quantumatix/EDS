# F010 – Product Reviews Simulator

**Feature ID:** F010

**Feature Name:** Product Reviews Simulator

**Capability:** Customer Experience

**Status:** Complete — final feature of Enterprise Data Simulator v1

---

# Objective

Generate realistic verified product reviews. A review is what a customer says
about something they actually received **and kept**: reviews originate only
from delivered shipment items that were not returned.

Generate `reviews.parquet`. Do not generate review votes, review replies,
moderation, or AI sentiment.

---

# Dependencies

Read only existing datasets; never regenerate them.

| Feature | Datasets |
| --- | --- |
| F001 | `products` |
| F002 | `customers` |
| F006 | `orders` |
| F008 | `shipments`, `shipment_items` |
| F009 | `return_items` |

The specification lists `customers.parquet` under F001; it is generated by
F002. The file is the same either way and the dependency is satisfied.

---

# Reviews

Fields: `review_id`, `review_number`, `shipment_item_id`, `shipment_id`,
`order_id`, `product_id`, `customer_id`, `rating`, `review_title`,
`review_text`, `verified_purchase`, `created_at`

## Review number

Format `REV-YYYYMMDD-000001`. Unique, sequential within a day, deterministic.

## Review eligibility

A shipment item qualifies when **both** hold:

| Condition | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| Shipment status | `DELIVERED` |
| Item in `return_items` | Absent |

At most one review per shipment item, at the configured rate.

## Verified purchase

Always `TRUE`. Every review originates from a delivered shipment, so there is
no unverified case to represent.

## Rating

Configuration driven, one to five stars:

| Rating | Share |
| --- | --- |
| ★★★★★ | 40% |
| ★★★★ | 30% |
| ★★★ | 15% |
| ★★ | 10% |
| ★ | 5% |

## Review titles and text

Both read from `reviews.yaml`, grouped by rating, and selected — never
generated. A three-star review can only carry three-star wording.

| Rating | Titles | Text |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ★★★★★ | Excellent Product, Highly Recommended, Loved It | "The product exceeded my expectations." |
| ★★★★ | Good Quality, Worth Buying | "The product is well made and does the job." |
| ★★★ | Average Experience, Meets Expectations | "The product is acceptable for the price." |
| ★★ | Could Be Better, Not As Expected | "The product is not quite what I expected." |
| ★ | Very Disappointed, Poor Quality | "The product did not meet expectations." |

Each body is one sentence. No AI-generated paragraphs.

---

# Timeline

```
Shipment Delivered → Customer Uses Product → Review Created
```

`created_at` must be after `shipment.delivered_at`, by a configured delay of
1–30 days.

---

# Configuration

[`configs/reviews.yaml`](../../../configs/reviews.yaml)

| Setting | Default | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `review_rate` | `0.18` | Share of eligible items reviewed |
| `rating_weights` | see above | Star rating shares; must sum to 1.0 |
| `titles` | see above | Candidate titles per rating |
| `texts` | see above | Candidate one-sentence bodies per rating |
| `min_review_days` / `max_review_days` | `1` / `30` | Delivery to review |
| `review_number_prefix` | `REV` | Leading token of the review number |
| `batch_size` | `100000` | Rows generated per batch |

`titles` and `texts` must cover every rating `rating_weights` can produce.
